## 2. Présentation longue — EN

**GRELECY — Green Legacy Community**

**Old hardware. New possibilities.**

Working graphics cards can still be useful as software requirements evolve. GRELECY is an independent initiative bringing together the people who own this hardware and those who want to help understand its limitations, document problems and explore practical ways forward.

Our starting focus is older NVIDIA GeForce hardware on Windows: Kepler-based GTX 600/700 models, Maxwell-based GTX 900 models and Pascal-based GeForce GTX 10 series models. Product series do not always identify the architecture, so we ask contributors to record their exact card and setup.

We are currently forming the community and gathering hardware information, experience and reproducible compatibility reports. Contributors will help assess possible directions and choose a first achievable technical objective. There are no GRELECY drivers, patches or supported software releases available at this stage.

GPU owners and testers can contribute observations and reproduce issues. Developers and researchers can bring experience in C/C++, Windows drivers, graphics APIs, GPU architectures, reverse engineering or diagnostic tools. Documentation, translation and community organization are equally welcome. You can also join to learn and follow the project.

GitHub is our starting point for introductions, reports, proposals and shared documentation. English is the main documentation language; French is also welcome.

GRELECY is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by NVIDIA.

Explore the project: https://github.com/FaipL/Grelecy

Introduce yourself: https://github.com/FaipL/Grelecy/issues/new?template=01-join.yml

## 4. Appel à volontaires — EN

**Still using a Kepler, Maxwell or Pascal GeForce on Windows? Help shape GRELECY.**

I'm building GRELECY — Green Legacy Community — to bring together people interested in keeping older graphics hardware useful.

The initial focus is Kepler-based GTX 600/700 cards, Maxwell-based GTX 900 cards and Pascal-based GeForce GTX 10 series cards. Exact models matter: not every GTX 600/700 uses Kepler.

We're looking for:

- **GPU owners and testers** to identify available hardware and share reproducible compatibility problems.
- **Developers and researchers** with experience in C/C++, Windows drivers, DirectX/Vulkan, GPU architectures, reverse engineering or diagnostic tooling.
- **Documentation and community contributors** to organize findings, translate information and welcome participants.

You don't need to be a developer or own a target GPU to join. Learning and following the project are welcome too.

We're starting with people, hardware and evidence. The community will help choose what to investigate first. There is no GRELECY driver or patch to download, and improvements are not guaranteed.

**To join, introduce yourself through the GitHub form:**
https://github.com/FaipL/Grelecy/issues/new?template=01-join.yml

Tell us your exact GPU and Windows setup if you have one, what interests you, and how you'd like to help. The introduction is public; share only details you're comfortable publishing.

Project overview: https://github.com/FaipL/Grelecy

GRELECY is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by NVIDIA.
