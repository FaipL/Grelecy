# GRELECY brand policy

**GRELECY** stands for **Green Legacy Community**: GRE for Green, LE for Legacy, and CY for Community.

## Identifying the project

Use **GRELECY** as the displayed project name and **Green Legacy Community** as its expanded form. Link to [the project repository](https://github.com/FaipL/Grelecy) when practical.

You may use the name to accurately refer to the project, credit its documentation, describe compatibility, or identify a fork's origin. Make the relationship clear and avoid implying official status, sponsorship, or endorsement.

A fork or independent service should use its own primary name and clearly explain that it is independent.

## Logos and visual identity

The selected design direction is **Hardware Legacy**, with an emblem inspired by a graphics card and the letter G. Final logo files are not included in this initial repository setup.

Unless an asset carries an explicit separate permission notice, rights in GRELECY logo artwork and visual assets are reserved by their respective rights holders. They are not included in the repository's MPL-2.0 or CC-BY-4.0 grants.

Request permission before presenting a logo as part of another project's identity, modifying it for branding, or using it on merchandise. Open a [brand-use request](https://github.com/FaipL/Grelecy/issues/new) describing the intended use and material; do not include private contact details.

## Third-party brands

GRELECY is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NVIDIA. References to NVIDIA, GeForce, Windows, and other third-party names identify the relevant products and their owners.

This policy does not assert trademark registration, restrict uses permitted by applicable law, or add restrictions to rights already granted by the software or documentation licenses. The prose of this policy is CC BY 4.0; the brand assets it describes are separate.
